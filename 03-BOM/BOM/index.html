    Bill of Materials

    Overview

    This is the bill of materials for my individual board (Driver Nano 3: Linear Actuator B + IMU). It matches the schematic; the reference designators are the ones on the schematic. Prices are US quantity-1 prices from my Component Selection page where I researched them. Rows marked "est." are estimates, and rows marked "lab stock" are common parts expected to come from the lab. The KiCad BOM export (BOM.csv) is inside the schematic ZIP.

    Cost summary

    New purchases for this board come to about $21.50 before shipping (see Component Selection, section 4), slightly above an even share ($17.50–$20.00) of the team's $70–80 budget. Passives, headers and the button are expected from lab stock; if they must be bought, they add a few dollars.
